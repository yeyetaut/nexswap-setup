Privacy

Your profile (first name, company, title, LinkedIn URL, interests and goals) is written to your NexSwap tag, which shares your first name, interests and goals with other tags nearby so you can be matched.

If you continue with Google, the same profile is saved to your NexSwap account (Google Firebase, Singapore region) so you can reuse it at future events. We use your Google sign-in only to identify your account; we don't store your email in your profile. You can delete your saved profile at any time below.

Without signing in, your profile is only kept in this browser on this phone.
