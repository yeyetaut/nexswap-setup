    Privacy

    Your profile (first name, company, title, LinkedIn URL, interests and goals) is written to your NexSwap tag, which shares your first name, interests and goals with other tags nearby so you can be matched.

    If you continue with Google, the same profile is saved to your NexSwap account (Google Firebase, Singapore region) so you can reuse it at future events. We use your Google sign-in only to identify your account; we don't store your email unless you choose to share it with your matches. You can delete your saved profile and matches at any time below.

    Your tag remembers who you matched with. When you sync, those matches are saved to your account, and your matches can see your name, company, title, LinkedIn, interests and goals, but only once you have both synced, so nobody can look you up without having actually met you. This page also remembers which tag is yours and a secret key that lets only your phone read your tag's matches.

    Without signing in, your profile is only kept in this browser on this phone and matches can't be saved.
