    Privacy

    Your answers (first name, hobbies, quick picks, what you're looking for, your Human Design type if you gave it, and who you are and would like to meet) are written to your NexSwap tag for this event. Only your first name shows on it.

    To find matches, your tag broadcasts your hobbies, quick picks, what you're looking for, your type and who you are and would like to meet over Bluetooth to the other tags here; anyone nearby with a Bluetooth scanner app could read them, but not your name. When your tag matches someone, it also broadcasts your first name, and both tags show each other's first name.

    Nothing is saved to an account. This phone remembers your answers so you can send them again quickly. The organiser resets every tag after the event, which clears your answers from it. This event is for people aged 18 and over.

    Your profile (first name, company, title, LinkedIn URL, interests and goals) is written to your NexSwap tag, which shares your first name, interests and goals with other tags nearby so you can be matched.

    If you continue with Google, the same profile is saved to your NexSwap account (Google Firebase, Singapore region) so you can reuse it at future events. We use your Google sign-in to identify your account, and save your Google email only for your matches to see, unless you untick “Share my email and LinkedIn with my matches”. You can delete your saved profile and matches at any time below.

    Your tag remembers who you matched with. When you sync, those matches are saved to your account, and your matches can see your name, company, title, interests and goals (and your email and LinkedIn, unless you turned that off), but only once you have both synced, so nobody can look you up without having actually met you. This page also remembers which tag is yours and a secret key that lets only your phone read your tag's matches.

    Without signing in, your profile is only kept in this browser on this phone and matches can't be saved.

    Sync over your tag's Wi-Fi

    1. On your tag, tap the menu button (top left), then Settings → Sync to phone.
    2. Scan the Wi-Fi code on the tag with your camera and tap Join.
    3. Come back here and tap Get my matches.
    Get my matches

    Your phone then goes back to its usual connection and your matches appear here.
